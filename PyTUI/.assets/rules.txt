DEBITO™ 

Players: 2
Deck: 54 cards (including Jokers)
Starting Debt™: 11 each
Lose Condition: 22+ Debt™
Debt Rule: Total Debt between players is constant. When one gains Debt™, the other loses the same amount.

Setup

Shuffle the deck.

Deal 4 cards to each player.

Turn Structure
1. Secret Exchange

Each player passes 1 card face down to the opponent.

Received cards are placed face up in the Bank.

Bank cards cannot be returned via exchange.

2. Play

Choose 1 card from hand or the Bank.

You may temporarily pick up Bank cards before choosing (to hide the source).

Play 1 card face down.

3. Reveal

Reveal simultaneously.

4. Boost (Optional)

After revealing, you may add matching-number cards from hand (pairs, triples, quads).

Face cards cannot be combined.

Combos:

Deal damage equal to total sum.

Apply Debt™ equal to the value of one card only.

5. Bank Effect

If you played a Bank card, discard 1 card from hand or Bank.
